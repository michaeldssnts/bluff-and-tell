# Bluff & Tell

**Know your friends. Believe nothing.**

A party game for 2–8 players. Play tricky truths, silly photo challenges, doodle-offs and silly audio challenges.

## How to play

1. Create a room and share its invite link or code.
2. Everyone joins on their own phone and chooses a nickname.
3. Pick 5, 10, 20 or 30 rounds. Play one challenge, vote, then see the results.
4. Tap Next together. Most points wins.

Correct truth guesses earn 2 points. Each person who picks your own lie earns you 1 point, and you get a 1-point bonus if nobody spots your truth.

Photos, doodles and jingles earn 2 points for each vote they receive.

## Features

- Private rooms with invite links and room codes.
- A party mix of text, photo, doodle and audio rounds.
- No timers: each step moves on as soon as everyone is ready.
- Quick or full games.
- Confetti and a shareable victory card at the end.
- A solo demo to try the game.
- Downloadable social cards to recommend the game.

## Run locally

Requires Node.js 22.13 or newer.

```sh
npm ci
npm start
```

Open http://localhost:4317 in your browser. Multiplayer play requires a connection between devices.
