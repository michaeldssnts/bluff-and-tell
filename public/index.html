THE GROUP CHAT, IN GAME FORM

A little truth.
A lot of bluff.

  1. Bring your people. One host, one room code, 2–8 players. Everyone uses their own phone.
  2. Play one round. Write a truth and a lie, copy a silly face, or draw a doodle. Your screen tells you what to do.
  3. Cast your vote. Pick the truth from four options, or vote for your favourite photo or doodle. You cannot vote for yourself.
  4. Enjoy the reveal. Correct guesses earn 2 points. Every person who picks your lie earns you 1 point, plus a 1-point bonus if nobody spots your truth. Each vote for your photo or doodle earns 2 points. Everyone sees the results before tapping Next together.

The featured player sits out their own truth question. Featured turns rotate. Choose the total number of rounds in the lobby; the time shown is an estimate.

KEEP IT IN THE ROOM

Your secrets,
your people.

Rooms are private by invite code. Anyone who has the code can join an unlocked lobby, so share it only with your group.

Your nickname, answers and votes are stored to run the game. Other players see your answer options during your question, and the truth and votes at the reveal. The host sees readiness counts, not your private answer sheet.

Rooms expire after 24 hours. Expired data is inaccessible immediately and removed from storage by the backend’s cleanup process. Your browser stores your room session and unfinished answers so you can reconnect. It also remembers recent questions and sends their IDs when you create or join a room to help reduce repeats. This history does not include your answers.

No advertising or analytics trackers are enabled in this version. We do not send your answers to an AI service.
