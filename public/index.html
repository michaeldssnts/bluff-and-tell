KEEP IT IN THE ROOM

Your secrets,
your people.

Rooms are private by invite code. Anyone who has the code can join an unlocked lobby, so share it only with your group.

Your nickname, answers and votes are stored to run the game. Other players see your answer options during your question, and the truth and votes at the reveal. The host sees readiness counts, not your private answer sheet.

Rooms expire after 24 hours. Expired data is inaccessible immediately and removed from storage by the backend’s cleanup process. Your browser stores your room session and unfinished answers so you can reconnect. It also remembers recent questions and sends their IDs when you create or join a room to help reduce repeats. This history does not include your answers.

No advertising or analytics trackers are enabled in this version. We do not send your answers to an AI service.
