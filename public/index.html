THE GROUP CHAT, IN GAME FORM

A little truth.
A lot of bluff.

  1. Bring your people. One host, one room code, 2–8 players. Everyone uses their own phone.
  2. Spill something real. Answer three prompts about yourself with one truth and one convincing lie.
  3. Trust absolutely nobody. Guess your friends’ truths from four options. The wildest one might be real.
  4. Enjoy the reveal. Correct guesses earn 2 points. Every person who picks your lie earns you 1 point.

The featured player sits out their own question. Everyone gets the same number of featured questions. Skip personal prompts you don’t want to answer.

KEEP IT IN THE ROOM

Your secrets,
your people.

Rooms are private by invite code. Anyone who has the code can join an unlocked lobby, so share it only with your group.

Your nickname, age group, answers and votes are stored to run the game. Your age group is used to choose prompts and is not shown to other players. Other players see your answer options during your question, and the truth and votes at the reveal. The host sees readiness counts, not your private answer sheet.

Rooms expire after 24 hours. Expired data is inaccessible immediately and removed from storage by the backend’s cleanup process. Your browser stores your room session and unfinished answers so you can reconnect.

No advertising or analytics trackers are enabled in this version. We do not send your answers to an AI service.
